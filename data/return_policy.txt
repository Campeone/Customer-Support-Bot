SHOPEASE - RETURN & REFUND POLICY

RETURN WINDOW
Customers have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. Items returned after 30 days will not be accepted unless they are defective.

ELIGIBLE ITEMS
Most items are eligible for return provided they are:
- Unused and in original condition
- In original packaging with all tags attached
- Accompanied by the original receipt or order confirmation

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
The following items cannot be returned:
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Perishable goods (food, flowers)
- Intimate apparel and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Customized or personalized items
- Hazardous materials

HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
1. Log in to your ShopEase account
2. Go to "My Orders" and select the order
3. Click "Return Item" and select your reason
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label
5. Drop off the package at any UPS location within 7 days

REFUNDS
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
- You will receive an email confirmation once your refund is processed

EXCHANGES
We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different size or color, return the original item and place a new order.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
If you receive a damaged or defective item, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund including shipping.

LATE OR MISSING REFUNDS
If you have not received your refund after 7 business days, first check with your bank. Processing times vary by institution. If the issue persists, contact support@shopease.com.
