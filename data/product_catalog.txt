SHOPEASE - PRODUCT CATALOG & POLICIES

PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Electronics
- Smartphones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, headphones, cameras
- All electronics come with a 1-year ShopEase warranty in addition to manufacturer warranty
- Electronics cannot be returned if opened, unless defective

Clothing & Apparel
- Men's, Women's, and Children's clothing, shoes, and accessories
- Sizes range from XS to 4XL for most items
- Shoes are available in US sizes 4–16
- Free returns on clothing items within 30 days

Home & Kitchen
- Furniture, cookware, bedding, decor, and small appliances
- Large furniture items ship via freight and require a signature upon delivery
- Assembly services available in select cities for $49.99

Sports & Outdoors
- Fitness equipment, outdoor gear, cycling, yoga, and team sports
- Large equipment (treadmills, weight benches) have a separate 14-day return window

Beauty & Personal Care
- Skincare, haircare, makeup, and wellness products
- Sealed items can be returned within 30 days
- Opened beauty products are non-returnable for hygiene reasons

PRODUCT WARRANTY

ShopEase Warranty covers manufacturing defects for 1 year from purchase date.
The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage from misuse or accidents
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from unauthorized modifications

To file a warranty claim, contact support@shopease.com with your order number and photos.

PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

Items marked "In Stock" ship within 1–2 business days.
Items marked "Pre-Order" will ship on the listed release date.
Items marked "Back Order" are temporarily out of stock — estimated restock dates are shown on the product page.

OUT-OF-STOCK NOTIFICATIONS
Click "Notify Me" on any out-of-stock product page to receive an email when it becomes available.
