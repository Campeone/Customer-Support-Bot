SHOPEASE - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is ShopEase?
A: ShopEase is an online retail store offering electronics, clothing, home goods, and accessories. We ship across the United States and to over 30 international countries.

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Visit shopease.com and click "Sign Up" in the top right corner. Enter your name, email address, and a secure password. You will receive a confirmation email within 2 minutes.

Q: Can I place an order without creating an account?
A: Yes. We support guest checkout. However, creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, and access your order history.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ShopEase Gift Cards.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. All transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology. We never store your full credit card number on our servers.

Q: Do you offer price matching?
A: Yes. If you find the same product at a lower price on a competitor's website within 7 days of purchase, contact support with a link to the competitor listing and we will match the price.

Q: How do I apply a discount code?
A: At checkout, enter your discount code in the "Promo Code" field and click "Apply." Discounts cannot be combined unless stated otherwise.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order?
A: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After 1 hour, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact support immediately if you need to cancel.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes. Gift wrapping is available for $4.99 per item. You can add a personalized message at checkout.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: You can reach us via live chat on our website (9am–9pm EST), email at support@shopease.com, or phone at 1-800-SHOPEASE (Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm EST).
