SHOPEASE - ORDER MANAGEMENT

CHECKING ORDER STATUS

You can check your order status by:
- Logging into your account at shopease.com and clicking "My Orders"
- Using the order lookup tool at shopease.com/orders with your order number and email
- Checking your shipping confirmation email for tracking details

ORDER STATUS DEFINITIONS
- Order Placed: We have received your order and payment is confirmed
- Processing: Your order is being picked and packed at our warehouse
- Shipped: Your order has left our warehouse and is with the carrier
- Out for Delivery: Your order is on the delivery vehicle today
- Delivered: Your order has been delivered to the address provided
- Cancelled: Your order has been cancelled and refund is being processed

MODIFYING AN ORDER

Orders can only be modified within 1 hour of placement. Modifications include:
- Changing the shipping address
- Updating item quantities
- Removing items from the order

To modify, contact support immediately via live chat for fastest response.

CANCELLING AN ORDER

To cancel an order:
1. Log into your account and go to "My Orders"
2. Select the order and click "Cancel Order" if the button is available
3. If the button is not available, the order is already in processing — contact support immediately

If an order has already shipped, you cannot cancel it. You will need to initiate a return once it is delivered.

MISSING ITEMS IN ORDER

If your order arrived with missing items:
1. Check your email — items may have shipped separately
2. Verify all items on your packing slip
3. Contact support@shopease.com within 7 days with your order number

DUPLICATE ORDERS

If you accidentally placed a duplicate order, contact support within 1 hour to cancel one. After 1 hour, you will need to return the duplicate order once delivered.

INVOICES AND RECEIPTS

Digital receipts are automatically sent to your email after purchase. To download an invoice:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "My Orders"
3. Select the order and click "Download Invoice"
