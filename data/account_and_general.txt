SHOPEASE - ACCOUNT AND GENERAL INFORMATION

FORGOTTEN PASSWORD
If you forgot your password:
1. Click "Sign In" on shopease.com
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your email for a reset link (valid for 24 hours)
5. Click the link and create a new password

CHANGING ACCOUNT DETAILS
To update your name, email, or phone number:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Account Settings"
3. Click "Edit Profile"
4. Make your changes and click "Save"
Note: Changing your email requires verification from both old and new email addresses.

DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT
To delete your ShopEase account:
- Email privacy@shopease.com with subject "Account Deletion Request"
- Include your registered email and reason for deletion
- Account deletion is processed within 7 business days
- All personal data is removed in compliance with GDPR and CCPA

LOYALTY PROGRAM - SHOPEASE REWARDS
ShopEase Rewards is our free loyalty program:
- Earn 1 point for every $1 spent
- 100 points = $1 reward credit
- Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity
- Bonus points offered during promotional events
- Redeem points at checkout under "Payment Methods"

PHYSICAL STORES
ShopEase is an online-only retailer. We do not have physical store locations.
However, we have partnered with select UPS and FedEx locations for:
- Package pickup (if you prefer not to receive at home)
- Return drop-offs

BUSINESS AND BULK ORDERS
For business or bulk orders (10+ units):
- Email business@shopease.com with your requirements
- A dedicated business account manager will be assigned
- Business accounts receive volume discounts of 10-25%
- Net 30 payment terms available for verified businesses

ACCESSIBILITY
ShopEase is committed to accessibility:
- Our website meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- Screen reader compatible
- Large text mode available in account settings
- Contact support@shopease.com for additional accessibility accommodations

PRIVACY AND DATA
ShopEase collects only necessary data to process orders and improve service.
We never sell your personal data to third parties.
To request a copy of your data, email privacy@shopease.com.
Full privacy policy available at shopease.com/privacy
