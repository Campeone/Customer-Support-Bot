SHOPEASE - SHIPPING INFORMATION

DOMESTIC SHIPPING (United States)

Standard Shipping (5–7 business days): $4.99
Expedited Shipping (2–3 business days): $12.99
Overnight Shipping (next business day): $24.99
Free Standard Shipping on all orders over $50.

Orders placed before 2pm EST Monday–Friday are processed same day.
Orders placed after 2pm EST or on weekends are processed the next business day.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

We ship to over 30 countries. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination.

Canada: 7–14 business days, starting at $14.99
United Kingdom: 10–18 business days, starting at $19.99
Australia: 12–21 business days, starting at $22.99
Other countries: Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees charged by their country.

ORDER TRACKING

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number. You can track your order by:
- Visiting shopease.com/track and entering your tracking number
- Logging into your account and viewing "My Orders"
- Clicking the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after the label is created.

SHIPPING DELAYS

ShopEase is not responsible for delays caused by weather, carrier issues, or customs clearance for international shipments. During peak seasons (November–December), please allow additional 2–3 business days.

LOST OR STOLEN PACKAGES

If your tracking shows "delivered" but you have not received your package:
1. Check around your property and with neighbors
2. Wait 24 hours as carriers sometimes mark packages early
3. Contact support@shopease.com with your order number if the issue persists
We will file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund.

PO BOXES AND APO/FPO ADDRESSES
We ship to PO Boxes via USPS Standard Shipping only. We also ship to APO/FPO military addresses with Standard Shipping (delivery times may vary).
