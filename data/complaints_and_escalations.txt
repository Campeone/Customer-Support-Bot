SHOPEASE - COMPLAINTS AND ESCALATION POLICY

WRONG ITEM RECEIVED
If you received the wrong item, please follow these steps:
1. Do not use or open the incorrect item
2. Take a photo of the item you received and the packaging
3. Log into your account and go to "My Orders"
4. Select the order and click "Report a Problem"
5. Choose "Received Wrong Item" from the dropdown
6. Upload your photos and submit the report
We will arrange a free return pickup and send the correct item within 2-3 business days, or issue a full refund if the item is out of stock.

SPEAKING TO A MANAGER
If you would like to escalate your concern to a manager:
- Request a manager via our live chat (9am-9pm EST)
- Email manager-escalations@shopease.com with your order number and concern
- Call 1-800-SHOPEASE and press 3 for manager escalation
A manager will respond within 4 business hours.

FILING A FORMAL COMPLAINT
To file a formal complaint:
1. Email complaints@shopease.com with a detailed description
2. Include your order number, date of purchase, and desired resolution
3. You will receive a complaint reference number within 1 hour
4. A senior agent will contact you within 24 hours

COMPENSATION POLICY
ShopEase may offer the following compensation for verified issues:
- Wrong item received: Free return + correct item shipped + 10% discount on next order
- Damaged item received: Full refund or replacement + 15% discount on next order
- Significant shipping delay (over 10 days late): 10% discount on next order
- Multiple issues with same order: Store credit up to $25

UNHAPPY WITH RESOLUTION
If you are unhappy with the resolution provided by our support team:
1. Reply to your support email with "ESCALATE" in the subject line
2. Your case will be reviewed by a senior manager within 24 hours
3. You can also contact our customer advocacy team at advocacy@shopease.com
